RESERVE BANK OF INDIA & DEPARTMENT OF FINANCIAL SERVICES
Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA) & Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)
Master Circular RBI/2019-20/86
Date: 10th June 2019

SUBJECT: Guidelines for Financial Inclusion Accounts and RuPay Debit Card Benefits.

CORE FEATURES:
1. No minimum balance requirement. Any balance from Rs. 0 upwards is maintained without punitive charges or maintenance fees.
2. Free RuPay Classic debit card with in-built accidental death and permanent disability insurance cover of Rs. 2,00,000 (subject to at least 1 successful financial or non-financial transaction within preceding 90 days).
3. Overdraft (OD) facility up to Rs. 10,000 available to one eligible earning member per household after 6 months of satisfactory account operation (no condition for OD up to Rs. 2,000).
4. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) credit of government subsidies without any deduction or lien.

SIMPLIFIED KYC NORMS (SMALL ACCOUNTS):
1. Individuals lacking standard Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) can open a "Small Account" by submitting a self-attested photograph and signature or thumb impression before an authorized bank official.
2. Small account limitations: Aggregate credits not to exceed Rs. 1,00,000 in a year; maximum balance at any time not to exceed Rs. 50,000; aggregate withdrawals not to exceed Rs. 10,000 in a month. Valid for 12 months, extendable by another 12 months upon proof of applying for an OVD.
