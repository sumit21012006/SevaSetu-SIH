MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE & FARMERS WELFARE / GOVT OF MAHARASHTRA
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi & Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi
Operational Guidelines & GR NSMN-2023/CR-56
Date: 30th May 2023

SUBJECT: Operational guidelines for direct financial assistance to farmer cultivator families.

OVERVIEW:
Under PM-KISAN, the Central Government provides financial benefit of Rs. 6,000 per year in 3 equal four-monthly instalments of Rs. 2,000 each.
Under Maharashtra Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi, an additional Rs. 6,000 per year is contributed by the State Government, making total annual benefit Rs. 12,000 per eligible farmer family.

ELIGIBILITY NORMS:
1. Small and marginal farmer families who hold cultivable land in their names as per land records of the respective State/UT.
2. The definition of family includes husband, wife, and minor children.
3. Land ownership records (Satbara 7/12 extract and 8-A holding) must be clearly mutated in the land registry.

EXCLUSIONS:
1. All institutional land holders.
2. Farmer families where one or more member belongs to:
   - Former and present holders of constitutional posts.
   - Former and present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, Mayors, Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
   - All serving or retired officers/employees of Central/State Govt Ministries, Departments, Public Sector Undertakings.
   - All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
   - Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, Architects registered with professional bodies.

MANDATORY PROCEDURES:
1. Aadhaar-based OTP or biometric e-KYC on the PM-KISAN portal.
2. Linking Aadhaar with bank account and active NPCI mapper configuration.
3. Land verification by Revenue Patwari / Talathi.
