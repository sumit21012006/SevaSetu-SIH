GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA
Higher & Technical Education Department / Social Justice Dept.
Government Resolution No.: EBC-2023/PK-118/Shikshan-2
Date: 15th July 2023

SUBJECT: Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme for SC, ST, VJNT, OBC, SBC, and EWS students admitted through CAP rounds.

PREAMBLE:
The scheme aims to reduce dropouts among disadvantaged students pursuing post-matriculation courses including Class 11-12, professional engineering, medical, pharmacy, polytechnic diplomas, and arts/science degrees in approved colleges across Maharashtra.

KEY PROVISIONS & CLAUSES:

Clause 1: Scope and Benefits
1. Full or partial tuition fee and examination fee waiver/reimbursement depending on caste category and course type.
2. Maintenance allowance (Day Scholar / Hosteller) credited directly to the student's Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
3. SC/ST students with family income up to Rs. 2.50 lakh receive 100% tuition and exam fee waiver.
4. OBC/EBC/SEBC students with family income up to Rs. 8.00 lakh admitted through Centralized Admission Process (CAP) receive 50% tuition and exam fee reimbursement.

Clause 2: Mandatory Eligibility Criteria
1. Domicile of Maharashtra State.
2. Completed Class 10 (SSC) examination with passing marks.
3. Admitted to a recognized institution through the official government CAP round (Management/NRI quota admissions ineligible).
4. Minimum 75% attendance record during the academic term.
5. Maximum two male children from the same parents are eligible (no restriction on female children).

Clause 3: Required Document Vault
1. Aadhaar Card (duly linked to bank account and active on NPCI mapping).
2. Domicile Certificate issued by Tahsildar / Sub-Divisional Officer.
3. Income Certificate issued by competent revenue authority (Tahsildar) for the current financial year.
4. Caste Certificate and Caste Validity Certificate (for professional course degree/diploma).
5. Non-Creamy Layer Certificate (mandatory for OBC / VJNT / SBC; valid for current academic year).
6. Class 10 Marksheet, Class 12 Marksheet, and previous qualifying exam marksheet.
7. College Admission Fee Receipt and Allotment Letter.
8. Self-Declaration regarding family income and number of beneficiaries.
