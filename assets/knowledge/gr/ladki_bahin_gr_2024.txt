SUBJECT: Implementation of "Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana" for economic empowerment of women.

PREAMBLE:
To provide economic freedom, health, and nutritional security to women in Maharashtra, the Government has sanctioned the "Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana". Under this scheme, eligible women receive direct financial assistance of Rs. 1,500 per month.

KEY PROVISIONS & CLAUSES:

Clause 1: Eligibility Criteria
1. The woman applicant must be a resident of Maharashtra state.
2. The woman applicant must be aged between 21 years completed and 65 years.
3. The cumulative annual income of all family members must not exceed Rs. 2.50 lakh.
4. The woman applicant must hold an individual bank account seeded with Aadhaar and active on the NPCI DBT mapper. Joint accounts are strictly prohibited.
5. Unmarried, married, widowed, divorced, deserted, and destitute women in the eligible age group qualify.

Clause 2: Ineligibility Conditions
1. If the combined annual family income exceeds Rs. 2.50 lakh.
2. If any family member is a regular/permanent employee in a government department, public sector undertaking, local body, or board (outsourced/contractual workers up to Rs. 2.5L income are exempt).
3. If any family member is an active Income Tax payer.
4. If the beneficiary family already receives more than Rs. 1,500 per month from any other government financial assistance or pension scheme (if less than Rs. 1,500, the differential amount is payable).
5. If the family owns a four-wheeler registered in their name (excluding tractors).

Clause 3: Mandatory Documentation
1. Aadhaar Card of the applicant.
2. Maharashtra Domicile Certificate OR 15-year Ration Card OR Voter Identity Card showing Maharashtra residence.
3. Income Certificate issued by Tahsildar / Competent Authority showing annual family income <= Rs. 2.50 lakh, OR valid Orange/Yellow Ration Card.
4. Passbook front page copy showing applicant's name, IFSC code, and account number.
5. Hamipatra (Self-declaration form undertaking compliance with non-taxpayer and non-government employee status).

Clause 4: Application Procedure & Scrutiny
1. Applications can be submitted online via ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in portal or the Nari Shakti Doot mobile application.
2. In-person submission is facilitated at Setu Suvidha Kendras, Gram Panchayats, Anganwadi Centres, and Ward offices free of cost.
3. Scrutiny is performed by the Anganwadi Sevika / Ward Committee within 15 days of receipt.
4. Approved beneficiaries receive monthly disbursements on the 15th of every calendar month directly into their Aadhaar-seeded accounts.
