MINISTRY OF EXTERNAL AFFAIRS - CPV DIVISION
Passport Rules 1980 & Tatkaal Scheme Documentation
Circular MEA-CPV-2023-T1
Date: 10th January 2023

SUBJECT: Norms for Fresh Passport Issuance and Expedited Tatkaal Processing.

CATEGORIES OF APPLICANTS:
1. Adult (Age 18 and above): 10-year validity passport.
2. Minor (Below 18 years): 5-year validity or until attaining 18 years of age.

TATKAAL APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS:
1. Under the Tatkaal scheme, passports are dispatched within 1 to 3 working days post-appointment at Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) without waiting for pre-police verification. Post-police verification is conducted.
2. Applicant must produce at least THREE documents from the following approved list of 13 documents:
   - Aadhaar Card / e-Aadhaar.
   - Voter Identity Card (EPIC).
   - Permanent Account Number (PAN Card).
   - Driving Licence.
   - Bank Passbook with photo from a Scheduled Public or Private Sector Bank.
   - Arms Licence.
   - Pension Payment Order.
   - Student Identity Card from a recognized educational institution.
   - Birth Certificate issued under the RBD Act.
   - Caste / SC / ST / OBC Certificate.

NON-ECR (EMIGRATION CHECK NOT REQUIRED) QUALIFICATION:
1. All persons having educational qualification of matriculation (Class 10 passing) and above.
2. Persons above 50 years of age.
3. Income tax payers and their spouse/children.
4. Children below 18 years of age.

POLICE VERIFICATION:
Clear police verification report regarding address residence proof and absence of pending criminal warrants.
